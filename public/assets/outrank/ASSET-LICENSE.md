The 112 files in this directory were downloaded from publicly accessible Outrank and third-party pages for visual-reference implementation. `manifest.json` maps each source filename or URL to a local asset. It also records assets that the original media downloader did not discover: the favicon, Apple icon, OG image, demo video, three writing thumbnails, and 20 testimonial portraits. The `.jpg` Vækster portrait is served as JPEG at its source despite its original `.png` filename. The Bright Brands portrait was re-encoded as a 256px JPEG to avoid shipping a 15 MB avatar.

After re-downloading, run `node scripts/download-testimonial-avatars.mjs`, `node scripts/normalize-outrank-assets.mjs`, and then `node scripts/audit-outrank-assets.mjs`. The latter checks that every asset is recorded, exists, has the expected file signature, and is non-empty. The 8.17 MB local MP4 is deliberately loaded with `preload="none"`; consider a licensed streaming/optimized delivery strategy before production.
